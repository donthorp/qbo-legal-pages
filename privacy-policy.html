Privacy Policy — QBO Balance Pull

Last updated: September 2026

"QBO Balance Pull" is a private, internal integration developed and operated solely by DM Holdings for the purpose of retrieving account balance information from DM Holdings' own connected QuickBooks Online companies. It is not distributed to, offered to, or used by any third party, and is not listed on the QuickBooks App Store.

Data accessed through this integration is limited to QuickBooks Online Chart of Accounts information (account names, types, and current balances) for company files that DM Holdings owns and administers. This data is stored in a private, access-controlled internal system operated by DM Holdings and is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party.

Because this application connects only to accounts under DM Holdings' own control, there is no separate "end user" whose data is being processed on behalf of anyone else.

Questions about this policy can be directed to don@dm-holdings.org.
